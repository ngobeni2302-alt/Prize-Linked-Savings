? How Prize-Linked Savings Works

1 Set a goal — Choose a target amount (e.g. R3,000) and a timeframe of at least 3 months.
2 Save consistently — Deposit via Pick n Pay, Shoprite, Boxer, PEP, Checkers, or Usave nationwide.
3 Complete your goal — No early withdrawal. After 3+ months you earn a guaranteed-win ticket.
4 Play your ticket — Every ticket is a guaranteed win: airtime, data, minutes (under R50), or group retail vouchers (5–20% off).
5 Refer friends — Share your code. When they complete their goal, you earn a free ticket.
i A −2.00% annual offset from your interest funds all tickets. Standard yields: 2.00%–5.50% p.a. (Personal) · 2.50%–6.00% p.a. (Group/Stokvel). Banking via African Bank.
